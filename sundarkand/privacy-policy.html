Sundarkand: Audio Paath

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

The short version. Sundarkand works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server. Your bookmarks, notes and practice log never leave your phone — we cannot see them, because they are never sent anywhere.

The one exception is advertising. The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, and Google collects data to serve them. Buying Remove Ads stops those requests entirely, after which the app makes no advertising network connections at all.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Sundarkand: Audio Paath app for iPhone and iPad ("the app"), published by Sourav Mishra ("we", "us"). It explains what happens to information when you use the app.

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no server, no account system and no analytics. We do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any record of what you read or listen to. There is no mechanism in the app by which we could.

Two third parties — Google and Apple — do process some information, as described in sections 4 and 5 below.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so it can work offline. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is never transmitted:

WhatWhy
Bookmarks and your private notesSo you can return to saved verses
Practice log — days you read, paaths completed, time spentTo show your personal sadhana record
Reading positionTo resume where you left off
Preferences — theme, text size, playback speed, reminder daysTo keep your settings between sessions
Whether you purchased Remove AdsTo keep ads switched off

The scripture text itself ships inside the app. Reading, searching and listening require no internet connection and generate no network activity.

Deleting this data: uninstalling the app permanently removes all of it from your device. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side and nothing to request from us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app displays ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • device information (model, operating system version, language, coarse region)
  • advertising and device identifiers
  • IP address, and general interaction data such as ad views and taps

This processing is carried out by Google as an independent party, under its own terms. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where ads appear — and where they never do

Ads are deliberately limited. A banner may appear on the section list, and an occasional full-screen ad may appear between sections at a natural pause. The reading and listening screen carries no advertising of any kind, including during continuous paath playback, and neither does the Settings screen. Ads are never shown in the middle of a verse or during audio.

Limiting ad tracking

The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not have access to Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). You can further limit personalised advertising in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising on your device.

The most complete option is the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Once it is active, the app stops requesting ads altogether and makes no connections to any advertising network.

5. Purchases (Apple)

Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase is valid. We never see your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no name. Refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple.

6. Notifications

If you enable the recitation-day reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. They are not push notifications, no message is sent through any server, and no token or identifier is shared with us. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

7. Widget

The "Chaupai of the Day" widget selects a verse using the date and content already stored on your device. It makes no network requests and reports nothing.

8. Children

The app is a devotional text intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it holds none from children either.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act give you rights to access, correct and delete personal data held about you. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase. Data stored on your device is under your control and is removed completely when you uninstall the app.

For data processed by Google for advertising, please exercise your rights directly with Google using the links in section 4.

10. Data security

Your notes, bookmarks and practice log are stored in the app's private container, protected by iOS app sandboxing and your device passcode or biometric lock. Since this information is never transmitted, it is not exposed to interception in transit or to a breach of any server we operate.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example if audio downloads or optional sync are added in a future version — this page will be updated and the date at the top revised. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
