Privacy Policy

Arrow Ink · Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Ink (the "app," "we," or "us") handles information when you use our iOS mobile application. We designed the app to collect as little data as possible. Please read this policy carefully.

1. Information We Collect

Directly from you: none. Arrow Ink does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, or any personal information, and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Stored on your device only: your game progress, settings, and purchase status are stored locally on your device using Apple's standard app storage (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device unless you enable iCloud backup for the app, in which case it is backed up under your Apple ID per Apple's privacy policy.

Collected by third-party advertising (Google AdMob): the app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following information to serve and measure ads:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, general location inferred from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, clicked, or completed)
  • Your Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt

If you do not grant tracking permission, AdMob still serves ads, but those ads are non-personalized and do not use your IDFA.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate the app and save your progress between sessions.
  • To process the optional "Remove Ads" in-app purchase (handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit — we never see your payment details).
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob, which uses the information described above.
  • To measure aggregate app performance and ad performance (via Google AdMob reports — we see aggregate stats, not individual user data).

3. Third-Party Services

Arrow Ink integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

  • Google AdMob — advertising. See Google Privacy Policy and AdMob privacy documentation.
  • Apple StoreKit — in-app purchases. See Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency — governs your ad-tracking choice, controllable at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS presents a prompt asking whether Arrow Ink may track your activity across other apps and websites for advertising purposes. Your choice controls whether Google AdMob may access your IDFA. You can change this choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Arrow Ink.

5. Children's Privacy

Arrow Ink is rated 4+ but is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app's advertising, please contact us and we will assist in removing it and instructing our ad partner to do the same.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal data held about you.
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal data for advertising purposes.
  • Withdraw consent for tracking at any time (via the iOS Tracking setting described above).

Because Arrow Ink itself does not collect personal data from you directly, most requests should be directed to our advertising partner (Google) using their privacy tools. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

7. Data Security

Because we do not operate a server or collect personal data ourselves, there is no user database on our side to secure or breach. Data handled by third-party services is protected under their respective security practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If changes are significant, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users through the app or the App Store listing before they take effect.
